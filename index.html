Connection

One database now. The gate machine sends every scan to the Cloudflare Worker, and the Worker writes the finished record here with the name, class, bus and staff category already attached.

No student or staff credentials needed. This page never touches those databases. Everything it shows comes from the attendance database below, so nothing sensitive lives in this file.

Attendance database

The same values as the Worker's RAW_FIREBASE_URL and RAW_FIREBASE_CODE variables. This is the only connection that reads and writes — settings you save here sync to app_settings.json, which the Worker reads on every scan.

Students

A student's day is split by one cutoff time: scan before it and it counts as arriving, at or after it and it counts as leaving. That works because students all arrive together.

Staff don't use this. Each staff category has its own arrival and departure windows over in Staff — a driver starting at 4am and a teacher arriving at 8:30 can't share a dividing line. The cutoff below is only a fallback for a category whose windows are left blank.

Cutoff time

Applies to every student, on every day, unless one of the overrides below says otherwise.

How the student view opens

Summary is the counts at the top, list is the register itself. This sets the device you're holding — to set another one, use Devices.

Different on certain weekdays

Half-day Saturdays, for example. One override per weekday — adding another replaces it.

Different on specific dates

Exam days, function days. Beats the weekday override for that date.

Calendar

Which days count. Non-working days and holidays are left out of monthly percentages, and the Worker skips marking anyone absent on them.

Working days

The school default. A staff category that works differently — security on all seven days, for instance — gets its own days under Staff shifts.

Sandwich off

A holiday or weekly off is paid on its own. It stops being paid when it is joined to an absence — a day off taken either side of it, or an absence sitting between two holidays.

Holidays

Fixed dates — festivals, breaks, a one-off closure.

Which staff categories — leave all unticked and it covers every category

Repeating holidays

Every second Saturday, for instance — applied to every month automatically.

Which staff categories — leave all unticked and it covers every category

Staff

Set two punch windows per category and nothing else. Say a teacher may scan in between 7 and 8 in the morning and out between 1 and 2 in the afternoon: both windows scanned is a full day, only one of them is a half day, and a scan that lands anywhere else — 12:00, say — is ignored, exactly as if it never happened. No shift timings to fill in.

Only one punch, or none inside the windows? One window scanned makes it a half day; nothing inside either window makes it an absence, even though the machine saw the person. Leave all four times blank and the category goes back to counting any scan as a full day. Each category can also keep its own working days.

Devices

Every phone, tablet and desk PC that opens the register lists itself here, the one you're holding first. Rename any of them, choose what each opens on, and hide staff categories a particular device shouldn't show — all of it from whichever device you happen to have in hand.

All devices

Changes save as you make them, and reach the other devices the next time they open the register.

Password

Locks this settings popup. The register itself stays open to everyone — only changes are protected.

Settings password

Stored as a scrambled fingerprint, not as the password itself, so nobody can read it out of the database. Forgetting it means clearing settingsPassHash from app_settings in the Firebase console.

Backup

Keep a copy of this configuration, or carry it to another device without typing it all again.

Export

Downloads one file holding the cutoff times, calendar, staff shift rules, device list and the database address.

Import

Reads a file exported from here and replaces everything on this device with it. Nothing is merged, and nothing reaches the gate machine until you press Save & sync.
