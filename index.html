Staff shift rules

By default a category just needs one scan to count as a full day. Switch a category to "Time-based shifts" to instead require scans inside specific from–to windows (e.g. Drivers/Cleaners with a morning and afternoon shift). Fill in Shift 2 only if that category works two shifts — both shifts scanned counts as a full day, one out of two counts as half day, and a scan outside both windows also counts as half day.
